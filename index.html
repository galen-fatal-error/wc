FIG. 04 · MONTE CARLO — 1000 FULL TOURNAMENTS

The odds table

Model: scorelines sampled from a Dixon-Coles-corrected bivariate Poisson — goal means set by the Elo difference (400-point logistic) with the low-score correction that fixes plain Poisson's habit of under-counting draws. Hosts (USA / Mexico / Canada) carry a home-advantage bump (~+70 Elo) in their group games. Group tiebreakers follow FIFA regulations — points, goal difference, goals scored, head-to-head; fair play approximated by lots. Third-place allocation solved per scenario against the published slot constraints. Ratings: World Football Elo (eloratings.net), June 2026 — a stronger match predictor than the FIFA ranking. Groups reflect the final draw and March 2026 playoff results — Bosnia & Herzegovina, Sweden, Türkiye, Czechia, DR Congo, Iraq. Part simulation, part entertainment — no wagering advice on this sheet.
