Group stage

Single round-robin. Top two from every group advance, plus the eight best third-placed sides ranked across all twelve groups — 32 of 48 go through.

FIG. 02 · PLACEMENT — GROUPS → ROUND OF 32, MATCHES 73–88

Out of the groups, onto the sheet

Every group position routes to a fixed round-of-32 slot. Winners and runners-up have predetermined paths; the eight qualified thirds are allocated against group winners according to FIFA's combination table — a third never meets the winner of its own group. FIG. 04 · MONTE CARLO — 1000 FULL TOURNAMENTS

The odds table

Model: match outcomes sampled from Poisson goal distributions tilted by FIFA ranking difference (600-point logistic, per FIFA's own SUM formula). Group tiebreakers follow FIFA regulations — points, goal difference, goals scored, head-to-head; fair play approximated by lots. Third-place allocation solved per scenario against the published slot constraints. Ratings: FIFA Men's World Ranking, April 2026 order (points approximated from the November 2025 release where unpublished). Groups reflect the final draw and March 2026 playoff results — Bosnia & Herzegovina, Sweden, Türkiye, Czechia, DR Congo, Iraq. Part simulation, part entertainment — no wagering advice on this sheet.
